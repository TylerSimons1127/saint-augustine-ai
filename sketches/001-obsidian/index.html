Why can't I find peace in the things I enjoy?
Augustine
Because the soul was made for its Source, and a creature cannot fill the shape of the Creator. The very unrest you feel is a signpost, not a sentence — it points home. As CCC §30 teaches, in his incarnation the Son of God has united himself in some fashion with every man.
