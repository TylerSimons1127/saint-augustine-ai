## Variant 3 — Atelier Glass (Multi-Panel Workspace)

**Stance:** A refined study product. Light glass, three panels: sessions rail · conversation · live **sources** rail. Turns Augustine's citations into a visible, hoverable feature.

**Key choices**
- Layout: 248px · 1fr · 272px glass panels, collapses to one column under 1000px
- Glass: blur(30px) saturate(180%) on every panel + composer; anchored "Augustine" label; deeper bubble shadow for hierarchy
- Motion: panel pop-ins, message pop, typing, live source row appears as you chat, hover states on sources + sessions
- User bubble = deep ink; gold = antique bronze (active states, citations, send)
- Sources rail: Roman-numeral citations, hover highlight, grows live as Augustine cites

**Trade-offs**
- Strong at: authority, making citations a feature, power-user workflow
- Weak at: densest layout; less "contemplative" than 1/2

**Best for:** seminarians / students who want the tradition *shown*, not just felt.
