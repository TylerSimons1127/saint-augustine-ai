# Saint Augustine AI — What's New

## v1.5 — The Premium Update
- **32-lesson curriculum** — a full month of daily-unique lessons; the bank no longer cycles every 8 days.
- **Command palette** — `Ctrl/Cmd+K` to jump to any page, lesson, or conversation.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** — `1-4` switch pages, `N` new conversation, `/` search, `S` settings.
- **Hash routing** — refresh/share deep links (`#study`, `#today`); your page survives reload.
- **Streak + progress strip** on Today — day streak, lessons read (with progress bar), quizzes done.
- **Lesson navigation** — Previous / Next / Browse all 32 lessons, with read-state (✓).
- **Glossary** — every lesson's key term, searchable, one tap from Study.
- **Illuminated drop caps** — each lesson opens like a manuscript page.
- **Pull-quote + Copy/Share** on every lesson quote.
- **Tomorrow's lesson teaser** at the bottom of Study.
- **Undo for deletions** — every conversation delete shows an Undo toast for 8 seconds.
- **Premium confirm dialogs** — native `confirm()` replaced with in-house glass modals.
- **Quiz mid-progress saved** — refresh mid-quiz and resume where you left off.
- **Branded loading states** — "Augustine pauses to consider…" while the model thinks.
- **Skeleton loaders** for readings and the saint of the day.
- **PWA** — installable app, offline shell via service worker.
- **Print styles** — lessons print cleanly as study sheets.
- **JSON backup/restore** — full data portability alongside Markdown export.
- **Mobile** — swipe from screen edge to switch tabs; iOS keyboard shows "send".
- **Fixed** — jump-to-latest pill (was dead markup), removed dead Darkness setting + header-rail CSS.
