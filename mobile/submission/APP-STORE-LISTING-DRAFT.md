## Description

Saint Augustine AI is an independent, experimental companion for exploring the writings and spiritual tradition of Augustine of Hippo.

CHAT — Bring questions about Augustine, Scripture, prayer, and the Christian life to a conversational AI inspired by his writings. Choose a shorter, thoughtful, or more contemplative response, then revisit or share conversations.

STUDY — Follow one of 32 lessons or choose a topic. Each lesson connects a topic with Augustine’s words, a source reading, vocabulary, and a writing to explore. Browse the glossary, keep notes, and take quizzes.

PRAY — Write a private intention, ask for a guided prayer or gentle instruction, save favorite prayers, and use a quiet prayer timer.

TODAY — Read the daily Mass readings, learn about the saint of the day, continue the daily lesson, and follow your study progress.

Conversations, notes, prayer intentions, and progress are stored on this device. Your messages, attachments, and prayer intentions are sent to the AI only when you choose to request a response. Requests are routed through our backend and OpenRouter to an AI model provider; providers’ retention and training practices vary. Review the Privacy Policy before using the AI features, and avoid sending information you consider highly sensitive. Sharing and backups happen only when you choose those actions. AI replies are generated and may be mistaken. They are not the historical Augustine or official Church teaching, and they do not replace a priest or confessor. An internet connection is needed for AI responses and refreshed daily content.

Created by Tyler Simons, an independent student project inspired by the Saint Augustine Study Club run by Fr. Bogumil.

## App Review notes draft

- No account or sign-in is required.
- The app’s interface and static content are bundled in the iOS app; it does not simply open the production website.
- Use the bottom navigation to explore Chat, Study, Pray, and Today.
- Chat streams responses from an AI service and requires an internet connection. The opening notice explains that replies are experimental and are not a priest or infallible teaching.
- Study includes the 32-part lesson sequence, selectable topics, source trail, glossary, notes, and quizzes.
- Pray includes on-device intentions, guided prayer flows, saved prayers, and a local timer.
- Today includes daily readings, the saint of the day, and on-device lesson and quiz progress.
- Conversation and answer sharing uses the iOS share sheet. Settings include a JSON backup and restore flow.
