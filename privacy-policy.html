Privacy · Saint Augustine AI

Your words, handled with care.

This page explains what stays on your device, what is sent when you ask for an AI response, and the choices available to you.

Last updated October 6, 2026

At a glance

Your conversations and progress are stored on this device. When you request an AI response, the app sends the relevant message and recent context through its backend and OpenRouter to a model provider. Providers’ retention and training practices vary; avoid sending information you consider highly sensitive.

What stays on your device

The app stores conversations, preferences, study progress, saved notes and passages, favorite prayers, prayer intentions, streaks, and quiz progress in local app storage. The app does not require an account, and this information is not automatically synchronized to a Saint Augustine AI account.

You can clear conversations and saved items in Settings, or uninstall the app to remove its local data, subject to your device’s own backup settings. If you export or restore a backup, that file is handled through the system share sheet or file picker and may contain conversations and other app data.

What is sent when you use AI

When you choose to send a message, the app sends its text and recent conversation context (up to 24 messages) to the Saint Augustine AI backend on Render. The backend forwards the request to OpenRouter and the model provider used for that response. If a selected model is unavailable, the backend may retry another supported model.

OpenRouter documents that model providers have different retention and training practices; some may retain inputs or outputs or use them for model improvement. The app does not control each provider’s independent practices and cannot promise that a provider will delete a request. See OpenRouter’s privacy policy and its provider directory for current details. Avoid sending information you consider highly sensitive.

A prayer intention is sent only when you choose a prayer action that uses it. The optional AI-style paste notice uses a local text-pattern heuristic, not a separate detection service. If you send a flagged message while the notice is active, the app also sends a boolean signal to our backend; it uses that signal to add brief context to the AI request so the reply can respond gently. The signal does not identify where the text came from.

Attachments and daily content

When you choose to send a PDF or text file, the app extracts its text on your device and includes that text in the AI request. When you send an image, its image data is included as selected and may contain embedded metadata. PDF.js is loaded from jsDelivr to read PDF files; the document is processed locally, and extracted text is sent only if you send the message.

For the saint-of-the-day summary and Augustine connection, the backend may send the public saint biography to OpenRouter and a model provider. This request does not include your conversations or personal prayer intentions. Daily readings and saint biographies are requested from their public source websites.

Service operation and other connections

The backend uses the network address of a request temporarily for rate limiting. It maintains aggregate request, model outcome, error, and feedback-category counters in memory; its application code does not put chat text in those counters. Hosting and AI providers may process ordinary connection and request metadata under their own terms.

The app loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. When you open an external citation, that site receives an ordinary web request. These requests disclose ordinary connection metadata; the app does not intentionally send chat text to the font or PDF.js asset services.

Sharing and your choices

Sharing a conversation, selected messages, or an exported file happens only after you choose to use the system share sheet. The recipient and any service you select may receive the content you share.

  • Clear conversations and remove saved items in Settings.
  • Disable the AI-style paste notice in Settings.
  • Avoid personal details in prompts and attachments.
  • Keep exported backups private; they may contain your local app data.

AI replies are generated and may be mistaken. They are not the historical Augustine or official Church teaching and do not replace a priest or confessor.

Retention and deletion

Saint Augustine AI does not maintain a user account or server-side conversation history in its application code. Conversations remain in local app storage unless you choose to share or send content for an AI response. After a request leaves the app, OpenRouter and the model provider process it to provide the response; their retention and deletion terms vary. We cannot guarantee deletion of copies retained by those services.

To remove local data, clear it in Settings or uninstall the app. For a question about the app or a privacy concern, use the project support link below. Do not include private prompts, prayer intentions, or other sensitive information in a public issue.

Questions or privacy concerns

Contact the project through GitHub Issues. Issues may be public, so do not post private conversations or personal information.
